# GoalWorld - Auditor Outreach Templates

This document contains the outreach templates to contact security auditors for the GoalWorld Solana Anchor program and off-chain infrastructure.

---

## 1. Neodyme Outreach Template

**Subject:** Security Audit Request: GoalWorld (Solana Anchor Program & Oracle)

**Body:**
```text
Hi Neodyme Team,

I hope this email finds you well.

We are preparing to launch GoalWorld, a decentralized prediction market platform built on Solana, and we would like to engage your team for a comprehensive security audit of our smart contracts and critical off-chain components.

Here is a brief overview of the project:
- Scope:
  1. Solana Anchor Program (approx. 1,500 lines of Rust)
  2. Off-chain Oracle & Vault Crank (TypeScript/Node.js, approx. 500 lines)
  3. SDK & Webapp integration points
- Repository: Private GitHub repository (access will be provided upon engagement)
- Target Timeline: We are targeting an audit kickoff in the next 2-3 weeks, aligning with our 8-12 week mainnet launch roadmap.

Could you please let us know:
1. Your current availability for a kickoff in July/August 2026.
2. Estimated pricing or your standard engagement model for a codebase of this size.
3. Any pre-requisites or documentation you require from our side before starting.

We look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
Nico
CEO, GoalWorld
```
